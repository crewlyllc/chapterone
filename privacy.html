When you sign up for our waitlist, we collect your email address and optionally your role (reader, creator, or both). We do not collect your name, IP address, or any other personal data unless you voluntarily provide it through our creator intake form.

Your email is stored via FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and may also be stored in our internal systems when the platform launches. We do not sell, share, or rent your personal data to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use FormSubmit for email capture during the pre-launch phase. Their privacy policy applies to data submitted through their service. We may use Google Forms as a fallback; Google's privacy policy would apply to those submissions.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us. We will honor all applicable privacy law requests (GDPR, CCPA, etc.).

Changes

This policy may be updated as the platform evolves. Material changes will be communicated to waitlist members.
